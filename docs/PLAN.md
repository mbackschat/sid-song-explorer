## Unified timeline and transport (`TIMELINE-3`)

On 2026-10-19 the user accepted the diagram of a shared transport/timeline panel with an attached proportional scrollbar and requested a functional browser POC. They subsequently required live updates while dragging, with the impression of one continuous timeline, and a button to return to the current heard position centered.

Required: one playback/zoom toolbar, voice mute/solo beside their lanes, and a scrollbar attached below the time ruler. Its nominal thumb fraction is visible duration divided by song duration; zoom and panel resizing change this ratio. Dragging the thumb, clicking the trough, arrow buttons and keyboard navigation browse without seeking. A normal voice/filter timeline click seeks playback and releases held inspection while keeping the clicked point in place. The visible range stays fixed when paused. In Scroll, a playhead left of center advances across a stationary timeline until reaching the middle, then centered scrolling begins. Automatic return to center during playback is required after clicks on either side; the [interaction principle](design/web-player-and-analyser.md#interaction-principle) does not cancel this requirement. The user clarified that a right-side clicked line must stay attached to the clicked note while it scrolls to center. During this transition the line marks the seek point, while the transport time and upcoming shading still report heard progress. The viewport scrolls at song speed. At center the line resumes heard tracking and the viewport catches up to current playback; this handoff shifts content forward by the elapsed return interval. There is no exponential easing or accelerated scroll during the approach. This clarified behavior supersedes earlier attempts to keep the moving line on heard time throughout. Fit song retains its whole-song range through seeking and advancing playback. Shift-click inspects the chosen point without seeking. Hold captures the current heard position without pausing sound. The held chart/settings remain fixed, while the playhead and upcoming shading follow current playback. The ruler also seeks on release. Follow releases held inspection and browsing, centers the current heard position immediately and resumes automatic following. Held inspector data remains captured when browsing another passage. Setup, loop editing and extended help expand on demand. Routine live-status/instruction rows are hidden; pending operations, errors, ending explanations, held details and trace warnings remain visible. The redundant navigation slogan is removed. Transient seek feedback and held details sit below the chart; seeking keeps toolbar geometry and accepted same-configuration observations stable.

During Follow, startup/ending may extend outside the tune to retain the centered playhead. The scrollbar clamps its position at these edges while keeping its nominal ratio; manual browsing constrains the viewport to the song. A view larger than the song uses a full disabled thumb. Files with unknown duration use a timeline limit labelled length unknown. Fit song displays zero through the known duration; **Fit timeline** captures the current limit on click when length is unknown and keeps its scale until explicitly fitted again. Both modes include prepared upcoming activity and disable manual scrolling until Scroll is selected.

The [time-label correction](validation/mono-poc.md#timeline-and-scrollbar-time-labels) keeps visible-window times attached to the chart and places full song endpoints beneath the scrollbar track; unknown duration is explicitly labelled length unknown.

The POC renders on pointer movement and reuses same-configuration observations while requesting nearby prepared data. Missing or unprepared observations remain shaded; continuous browsing does not imply unlimited retained data or extra emulation. [The validation record](validation/mono-poc.md#unified-timeline-panel) owns guards, performance scope and browser evidence. Status: local implementation verified; the user explicitly confirmed on 2026-10-20 that the playhead handoff works and is accepted. On 2026-10-20 the user authorized completing the clicked-note correction and continuing through the remaining plan without waiting between work items. This continuation is not a listening or visual acceptance verdict. Public deployment remains outside this POC.
